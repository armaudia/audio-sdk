## Checkpoint and runtime

The pinned one-step checkpoint is
`logs/CT_pesq5e-4_L2/8ar2h6zv/last.ckpt` (Hugging Face layout), recorded in the
external research ledger as `data/models/rose-cd/last.ckpt`, **1,313,064,415
bytes**, SHA-256
`5f4fcf08b4f751b1c6d5d7575522a633327302aee0de4613b45d98f7285637a4`. The bytes
are **absent** from the external research checkout and this SDK worktree.

The runtime is the upstream PyTorch/PyTorch-Lightning Python 3.11 environment
with the legacy metric import graph described by `adapters/rose-cd/README.md`.
It is not installed, pinned by wheel/sdist digest or executed here.

Artifact-specific checkpoint redistribution and product-use rights, the
VoiceBank-DEMAND/EARS-WHAM training-data rights chain and legal review are
**not established**. No checkpoint, source, graph or runtime is downloaded,
copied, built, vendored, imported, loaded or redistributed by this package.

## Synthetic stimuli

The three deterministic synthetic signals (clean voiced surrogate, two
band-limited noise realizations, silence) and the impulse are generated by
`reference.py`. They are not recorded speech, not a corpus, not a room capture
and not a source-reported ROSE-CD output.

## Separation from other ROSE-CD work

The pinned research reports, asset ledger, candidate census and
`adapters/rose-cd/` are external provenance only. They were not executed,
imported, pooled or reinterpreted here. The sibling RMS-matched configuration
is explicitly out of scope; this package models only the raw pinned one-step
checkpoint. The EARS blind screen uses CC BY-NC 4.0 research material; only its
published report is pinned, and no EARS audio is read.
